Who's cutting
your story

A short introduction — the kind you'd get on a first call, not a resume.

I'm Farhan Anjum Sami, a video editor working with brands and agencies on corporate films and commercial ads. My job starts where the footage ends — finding the story buried in hours of raw clips and cutting it down to something that actually holds attention.

I work fast without cutting corners: clean pacing, motion graphics that support the message instead of decorating it, color grading that matches the brand, and sound design that carries the emotional weight most edits skip.

Every brief is different, so every cut is built from scratch for the client, the platform, and the fifteen seconds you actually have before someone scrolls past.

How a project
moves

The workflow every commercial or corporate edit goes through, start to delivery.

01

Brief & footage review

Understanding the brand, the goal, and what the raw footage actually gives me to work with.

02

Story assembly

A rough cut built around the strongest moments and a clear beginning, middle, and end.

03

Fine cut & motion graphics

Tightening pace, adding titles, lower-thirds, and any graphics the brand needs.

04

Color & sound

Grading for brand consistency and mixing music, VO, and sound design into one clean track.

05

Delivery

Exporting in every format and aspect ratio the campaign needs, with revisions included.
